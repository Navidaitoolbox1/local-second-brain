🗂️ Local Notion Dashboard

A free, local, open-source alternative to Notion. Your files never leave your machine — everything runs in this page.

Open your vault to begin

Click Open Vault Folder and select your Local Notion folder (the repository root). The dashboard scans your Markdown files locally and shows tasks, projects, and notes.

🔒 100% offline — no network requests, no uploads, no tracking. Reading is read-only; nothing in your vault is modified.
