# Roadmap to master Claude

A simple path from first login to advanced use. Follow the Quick Start track first. Move to Go Deeper when you want more.

## Quick Start

| Step | Time | What you learn |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Claude** | 5 min | Where to type, what to ask first, and what every button on the screen actually does. |
| **Prompt Claude properly** | 5 min | The simple structure that turns a vague request into a sharp, useful answer. |
| **Get Claude certified (free)** | 4 min | The steps to earn an official Claude certificate you can put on LinkedIn. |
| **Use Claude's new design** | 9 min | A quick tour of the redesigned interface so you stop hunting for things. |
| **Create slides with Claude** | 7 min | Go from a rough idea to a finished deck without ever opening PowerPoint. |
| **Build your first Claude skill** | 6 min | Teach Claude a repeatable task once, so you never have to re-explain it. |
| **Stop Claude from agreeing** | 5 min | Make Claude push back, challenge your thinking, and tell you when you are wrong. |
| **Let Claude use your computer** | 4 min | Give Claude permission to click, type, and navigate your screen on your behalf. |

## Go Deeper

| Step | Time | What you learn |
|---|---|---|
| **Use Claude Cowork** | 18 min | Turn Claude into a real workspace where your files, chats, and projects live. |
| **Organise work in Projects** | 10 min | Group related chats and files so Claude has the right context. |
| **Set up your team on Claude** | 11 min | Bring colleagues onto Claude, set roles, and share work. |
| **Train Claude to sound like you** | 12 min | Feed Claude your writing so it drafts in your voice. |
| **Stop hitting usage limits** | 12 min | Practical ways to stretch your Claude usage further. |
| **Move past basic prompting** | 10 min | What comes after prompts: designing repeatable workflows that prompt themselves. |
| **Stop sounding like AI** | 20 min | The specific phrases and patterns that scream "AI wrote this" and how to drop them. |
| **Build a tool with Claude Code** | 11 min | Describe a small personal tool out loud and have Claude build it for you. |
